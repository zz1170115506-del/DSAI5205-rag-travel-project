Your question→Retrieve evidence→Rank & filter→Answer & sources

Retrieval combines SQLite BM25 with optional local BGE embeddings and reciprocal-rank fusion. Area and category constraints filter results. Smart Assist uses the retrieved evidence to answer with citations.

AMap supplements missing named places and category searches. External candidates remain unreviewed and are not automatically added to the planner. Walking routes use provider-resolved coordinates, separate from the approximate itinerary overview.

Check official sources for fares, opening hours, weather and ferry services. AMap coverage and permissions depend on the configured account. Service readiness is shown above.
